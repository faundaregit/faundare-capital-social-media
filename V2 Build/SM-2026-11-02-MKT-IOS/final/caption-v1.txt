A data center is not just a building project. It is a logistics event.

S&P Global reports that every 1 GW of U.S. data-center expansion requires an estimated 100,000 truckloads—generators, transformers, cooling systems, steel and more.

That can create a second-order real-estate opportunity: permitted, truck-accessible outdoor storage and staging space near active construction clusters.

But proximity alone is not an investment thesis. Before underwriting a premium, verify the active project pipeline, zoning, truck access, hardstand capacity, lease duration and whether demand continues after construction.

The obvious asset is the data center. The investable question may be what the buildout needs next door.

Source: S&P Global Market Intelligence, Aug. 7, 2026. Truckload estimate attributed to DAT Freight & Analytics.
